# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, evidence-oriented community where
people can contribute regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully and assume good intent while evaluating claims
  critically;
- welcome questions from people with different disciplinary and technical
  backgrounds;
- distinguish evidence, interpretation, opinion, and organizational
  endorsement;
- give actionable feedback and acknowledge contributions;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest;
- respect privacy, confidentiality, attribution, and licensing;
- accept correction and take responsibility for mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are unacceptable:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory language;
- sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention;
- personal attacks, insults, trolling, or sustained disruption;
- publishing private information without permission;
- misrepresenting another person's views, affiliation, or endorsement;
- retaliation against people who raise concerns;
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  scientific or open-source setting.

## Scope

This Code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
events, private project communications, and public spaces where someone is
representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary technical disagreement, use the relevant issue or pull request.
For conduct concerns, contact the repository owner privately through the contact
route published on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/dhuzard)
or associated profile website. Do not post sensitive details in a public issue.

Reports will be reviewed promptly, impartially, and with attention to the safety
and privacy of everyone involved. Project maintainers may edit or remove
contributions, issue warnings, restrict participation, temporarily suspend
access, or permanently exclude a participant. Enforcement decisions will avoid
unnecessary disclosure and may be appealed under
[`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md).

Knowingly false or retaliatory reports may themselves violate this Code, but an
unsubstantiated good-faith report will not be penalized.
