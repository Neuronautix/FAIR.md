example. It contains no personal, sensitive, or third-party data.

- Retrieval uses public HTTPS with no authentication.
- The CSV is machine-readable and editable with open-source tools.
- Metadata explicitly link to the CSV distribution.
- CC0-1.0 is declared for the synthetic content.
- No DOI, research catalog record, formal preservation policy, or PROV-O graph
  is claimed.

This evidence supports demonstration of the v0.3 assessment structure; it is not
an independent FAIR assessment or certification.
